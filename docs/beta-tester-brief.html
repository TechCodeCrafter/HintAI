Tester brief — know before you answer

Thanks for helping us test MeetHint in a real meeting or realistic rehearsal. Plan about 30 minutes for setup, then use it in one conversation where cited answers would actually help.

What we need you to test

  • Cite or silence — answers should come from your material with citations, or stay silent when the files don’t support an answer.
  • Live during a call — mic + (on desktop) meeting tab audio, while you ask technical questions out loud or by typing.
  • Your own sources — repo, folder, PDF, Word, PowerPoint, or spreadsheet you’re allowed to use — not the demo pack.
  • Honest feedback — 👍 / 👎 on answers, plus a short note on what worked or broke trust.

Supported devices

Chrome / Edge
Best experience — Live with tab audio + mic, auto-answer when speech is detected.
Safari (Mac)
Live with mic; tab audio is limited — type or speak questions manually.
iPhone / iPad
Ask + Search with mic; tap Answer when the green dot appears. Full tab-audio Live needs desktop Chrome.

Steps (about 30 min)

  1. Sign in at meethint.ai with Sign in with Google (no invite code needed).
  2. Create a Knowledge Space named after a real project or meeting.
  3. Add your material (repo, folder, PDF, Office file, etc.) and wait for indexing to finish.
  4. In Live, select your Knowledge Space — not Demo pack (sample only).
  5. Ask one test question you’d ask in a real meeting (e.g. “What owns authentication?” or “Where is the refund policy?”).
  6. Start Live and use MeetHint in one real or realistic call for at least 10–15 minutes.
  7. Rate answers with 👍 Useful or 👎 Not useful (pick a reason if asked).

Please tell us

  1. Did any answer feel confident but wrong? (Copy the question + what it said.)
  2. Did MeetHint stay silent when it should have, or speak when it shouldn’t?
  3. Were citations useful — did they point to the right file/line?
  4. What was the biggest friction (setup, indexing, Live, latency, UI)?
  5. Would you use this in your next technical meeting? Why or why not?

Optional (helps a lot)

From Live → Diagnostics → download the JSON export and send it with your notes. It contains timings and quality metrics only — not your source file contents.

Privacy

Your files stay on your device in the browser. Signing in on another computer does not copy uploads — add material again on each device. We only receive what you send us (feedback, email, or a diagnostic export you choose to share).

Problems? Reply to your MeetHint contact. If something blocks you entirely (sign-in, wrong account data, crash loop), put BLOCKER in the subject so we prioritize it.
