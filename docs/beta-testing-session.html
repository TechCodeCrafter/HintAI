Get started

  1. Go to meethint.ai → Sign in with Google. Use the account you will keep for this test.
  2. No invite code needed — your account is created when you sign up.
  3. Confirm home shows Get ready for your first meeting.
  4. Reply to us with the Google email you used.
Before testing: Load your own repo, folder, PDF, or Office files — not Demo pack (sample only). Write 3–5 questions your docs can answer and 2–3 they cannot. Best setup: Chrome or Edge on desktop · mic + meeting tab audio.
